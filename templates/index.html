🧠 Parkinson’s Handwriting Detection AI

A healthcare-oriented deep learning system built using TensorFlow and Flask to help analyze handwriting patterns and assist in early Parkinson’s screening.

What is Parkinson’s Disease?

Parkinson’s disease (PD) is a neurodegenerative disorder that affects movement control. It often causes tremors, stiffness, and difficulty with handwriting, known as micrographia—where handwriting becomes small and cramped.

Why Handwriting?

Changes in handwriting are one of the earliest visible signs of Parkinson’s. By analyzing these patterns with artificial intelligence, we can potentially assist in early detection and clinical research support.

How This Project Works

  • Uses a pre-trained MobileNetV2 CNN model fine-tuned on handwriting samples.
  • Processes the uploaded handwriting image to detect Parkinson’s-like patterns.
  • Outputs prediction (Healthy / Parkinson) with confidence percentage.
  • Serves as an AI-assisted healthcare screening tool.
